01Reported acquisitionClassic Mile: scale in the equestrian corridorReported transaction: Jul 16, 2024US$26.9Mreported purchase price · 2024＋

The project

Ocala-News reported a US$26.9M purchase by Golden Ocala Equestrian Land LLC from Martelli Farm LLC, citing a deed dated July 16, 2024. An earlier county application identifies 703.98 acres within parcels 21317-000-00 and 21317-000-01.

What it brings to Ocala

The acquisition expands Golden Ocala Equestrian Land's presence along Highway 40, in the equestrian corridor surrounding Rainbow Park.

Status and scope

Acquisition reported by the press based on public records. The 2024 planning application dates to Martelli's ownership and places the property within the Farmland Preservation Area. The buyer's future program will be added when published.
