01Conditional approvalWEC's sports expansionMarch 2026 approvals250.86acres in the land-use change for the expansion＋

North of SR 40, between NW 100th and NW 80th Avenue

The project

The plan published by the county includes multipurpose and baseball/softball fields, a championship field and an indoor venue for ice sports, basketball, volleyball and soccer. The 250.86 acres relate to a residential-to-WEC land-use change; the complete combined PUD covers 4,276.21 acres and 391 parcels.

What it brings to Ocala

A larger offering of competitions and events could increase activity from visitors, teams and services in west Ocala.

Status and scope

The study identifies transportation needs. Land-use approval does not equal full construction permits or certification of road capacity. Legal challenges are documented in April and July 2026; their final resolution and a firm opening date were not verified.
